# UX rubric

The UX specialist judges every page and journey against these, at each viewport (desktop 1440, laptop 1024,
phone 390) and colour scheme. A failure of 1–4 is at least major; the rest are minor unless they block a task.

1. **It works without a mouse.** Every control is reachable in a sensible tab order, with a visible focus ring;
   dialogs and drawers trap focus and return it.
2. **It reads.** Text contrast meets WCAG AA on every background, gradients and images included; nothing is
   clipped, overlapped or cut mid-word at any width.
3. **It says what's happening.** Loading, empty, error and success states exist and say what to do next.
4. **It fits.** No sideways scroll; touch targets at least 44 px on phone; nothing important below a fold it
   shouldn't be.
5. **It's consistent.** The project's components, tokens and spacing, not one-off styles; the same thing looks
   and behaves the same everywhere.
6. **It's honest and kind.** Plain words, no dark patterns, nothing that blames the person.
7. **It respects motion and data.** Reduced motion is honoured; pages stay usable on a slow phone.
